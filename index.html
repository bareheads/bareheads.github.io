05 / Inner World

On Life & Work

What I've learned so far.

The Best Things Are Free — But Not Easy

I believe the best things in life are free: knowledge, curiosity, the feeling of a forest after rain, the bond with a horse or a dog, the quiet satisfaction of understanding something complex. But free doesn't mean easy. Learning demands effort. Growth demands discomfort. Trust demands vulnerability. The things that matter most cost nothing, but they ask everything.

On Science & Wonder

Every time I learn something new about the world, whether it's maths, physics, biology, or something else, I feel a sense of wonder; "How did someone come up with that?" Science isn't just facts and formulas — it's a way of seeing. The universe is stranger and more beautiful than I imagined. That's enough for a lifetime of curiosity.

On Growth & Discomfort

Challenging yourself and your own views is the key to happiness and a lot more. I've seen it in my own career: every time I stepped out of my comfort zone — from traditional HR into data driven approach, from Excel spreadsheets to SQL — I was uncomfortable at first. Then I grew. Then I couldn't imagine going back. Comfort is a slow trap.

On Lifelong Learning

I don't see learning as a phase or a requirement — it's a practice, like breathing. M.Ed. was an important step, but most of my education has been self-taught: coding at home after a long day at work, going down countless literary rabbit holes for curiosity's sake only, learning guitar by ear. Learning seems to be an itch I cannot scracth.

On Connection & Nature

Animals and nature have taught me more than any business book. My horse doesn't care about my job title. The forest doesn't care about my LinkedIn connections. Nature is honest in a way that human systems rarely are — you give attention and it responds. That's the model I try to live by.

On Culture & Meaning

Music, films, literature, art - these are my tools to process being human. Culture is a language for the things we feel but can't easily say. I play guitar because it takes me somewhere words cannot. I watch movies because they let me live a thousand lives. I read because every book is a conversation with someone who thought deeply about something I care about.
